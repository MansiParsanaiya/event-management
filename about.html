The EventPro Story

Founded in 2018 by three frustrated event managers, EventPro was born out of the need for a seamless, transparent, and human-centric platform. We realized that existing systems were too complex and failed to put the attendee experience first.

Today, we've grown into a global team of developers, designers, and event professionals, committed to simplifying the entire event lifecycle—from initial marketing to post-event analysis. **We believe technology should enhance, not complicate, the joy of gathering.**
